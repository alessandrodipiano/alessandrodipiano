# Hi, I'm Alessandro 👋

I'm a **B.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence** interested in the intersection of **machine learning, computer vision, robotics, human motion analysis, and quantitative medical imaging**.

My work focuses on applying mathematical and computational methods to real-world scientific and engineering problems, with particular interest in **perception, predictive modelling, statistical learning, and intelligent systems operating under uncertainty**.

## Research Experience

### Human-Aware Robotics

I am currently working on **human-aware motion planning for collaborative robotics** at Chung Yuan Christian University in Taiwan.

The project focuses on a **FANUC CRX-10iA/L collaborative manipulator** operating in a shared human–robot workspace. I am developing and evaluating the system in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, integrating:

* RGB-D perception and dynamic obstacle tracking
* Robot kinematics and full-body collision modelling
* Reactive motion generation
* Human and robot velocity estimation
* Predictive collision-risk assessment
* Short-horizon clearance prediction
* Safety behaviours such as avoidance, yielding, and retreat

The longer-term objective is to transfer the framework from simulation to the physical FANUC platform.

### Quantitative Medical Imaging

I also conduct research in quantitative MRI in collaboration with **IRCCS Policlinico San Matteo Hospital** in Pavia.

The work investigates whether information derived from **IVIM-DWI diffusion MRI** can be used to predict pharmacokinetic biomarkers obtained from **DCE-MRI**, with particular attention to \(K^{trans}\).

The project involves cross-modality image preprocessing, spatial alignment, statistical analysis, predictive modelling, and robust evaluation under **small-sample clinical-data constraints**. I have explored both statistical and deep-learning approaches, including regularised regression, gradient-boosted trees, and U-Net-based models.
